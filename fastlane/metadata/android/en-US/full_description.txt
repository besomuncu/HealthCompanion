A basic M3 styled app to track your water intake/medications/blood pressure checks polished for everyone with it's detailed accessibility settings! With an option for a simplified layout!

With this app, you will be able to:
-Track your Blood Pressure over the days (manual entries)
-Track your medications with notifications
-Track your liquid intake (manual entries)
-Share your user profile and data with your caretakers if needed.

This app will get updated over the days too!
